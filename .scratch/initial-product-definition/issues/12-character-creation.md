# 12 — Character creation

**What to build:** A confirmed member creates their first character: a display name (spaces and symbols allowed, uniqueness not required) and a randomly assigned unique username.

**Blocked by:** 05 — Login, logout & sessions

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Character created with the chosen name; names are not required to be unique
- [ ] Random username is unique per forum (invariant 2) and used in mentions, searches, and URLs
- [ ] The character appears under the member's account
